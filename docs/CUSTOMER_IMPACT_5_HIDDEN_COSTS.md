## 1. Executive Summary: Quantifying the Hidden Payroll & Pipeline Hygiene Iceberg

Direct runner compute is only the visible tip of a repository's CI/CD expenditure. Across production engineering organizations, **five hidden payroll and pipeline hygiene cost dimensions** routinely outweigh raw runner compute by **2x to 46x**:

1. **`developer_wait_time_cost` (Developer Wait Time & 40% Context-Switch Loss):** Critical-path DAG duration blocks pull-request merges and forces developers into context switches. Applying the empirical **40% context-switch productivity loss factor** (`productivity_loss_factor = 0.40`) turns raw critical-path wait hours into verifiable productive engineering payroll loss (`$65/hr` base rate and `$100/hr` blended fully loaded rate), along with a `wait_to_compute_cost_ratio` and a `lead_time_feedback_tier` classification (`best-in-class (<5m)`, `tight-feedback (5-10m)`, `moderate-drag (10-30m)`, `severe-context-switch-drag (>30m)`).
2. **`failed_build_rerun_cost` (Flake Rerun Compute + 8-Minute Investigation Payroll):** Flaky CI failures (`5.0%` baseline on single-OS pipelines; `10.0%` on multi-OS / matrix pipelines) trigger full or job-level reruns plus an average **8.0 minutes of engineer triage/investigation per flake** (`avg_investigation_mins_per_flake = 8.0`). `minici` separates immediate rerun compute waste from developer investigation payroll and quantifies `monthly_savings_at_1pct_target_usd`.
3. **`build_cache_savings` (Per-Stack Build Cache Coverage & Unrealized Savings):** Fingerprints the repository's primary build stack (`Node / npm`, `Node / pnpm`, `Rust / Cargo`, `Python / Poetry`, `Python / pip`, `iOS / Xcode DerivedData`), audits every expanded job for cache configuration (`actions/cache`, setup-lang cache flags, `restore-keys`, Docker BuildKit GHA cache), and computes exact `monthly_uncached_compute_usd` and `monthly_unrealized_cache_savings_usd`.
4. **`flaky_test_ci_cd_tax` (Combined Flaky Test Velocity Tax & Deflake Payback Weeks):** Models the compounding downstream drag of flaky tests—combining immediate rerun + triage cost (`1x`) with a **2.0x deferred velocity drag multiplier** (`deferred_velocity_drag_multiplier = 2.0`, capturing blocked merge queues, broken batching, and eroded CI trust) and calculating the exact **payback period in weeks** (`deflake_payback_weeks`) for a standard `$4,000` engineering deflake sprint.
5. **`branch_protection_cost` (Branch Protection Check Multiplication, Concurrency Cancel & Path Filter Savings):** Audits total required check executions (`required_checks_count × 433 runs/mo`), workflows missing `concurrency: cancel-in-progress: true` (`monthly_superseded_run_waste_usd`), and workflows missing selective `paths:` / `paths-ignore:` filters (`monthly_unfiltered_check_savings_usd`), while recognizing monorepo graph tools (`turbo`, `nx`, `bazel`) where present.

All five dimensions are integrated directly into `minici`'s **single unified product output** (`--format table`, `--format json`, `--format markdown`, `--format html`, and GitHub Action PR comments) under the **Strict Applicability Rule**—ensuring zero hypothetical bloat and surfacing only actionable, repo-specific findings.
